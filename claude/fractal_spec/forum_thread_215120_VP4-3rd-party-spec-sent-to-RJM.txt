Communications with the editor wouldn't be "3rd party MIDI support".

Fractal's proprietary comms protocol was introduced for so-called 'clients': editor and FC controllers through FASLINK 2.

The VP4 isn't such a client, as I see it. It's a standalone device designed for traditional pedalboards, supporting other MIDI-enabled pedals if needed, and doesn't support FASLINK.

I'm not saying there's no Sysex under the hood. But it's not necessary to control the VP4.

That can be done through regular MIDI: presets, scenes, channels, effects, etc. Perhaps not everything, like switching modes.

The discussion goes back to the introductory thread and afterwards, i.e. when the Send MIDI block was added.
